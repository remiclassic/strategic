# Duskbound Raiders

Dark fantasy with chunky low-poly 3D styling for casual portrait mobile action RPGs. Palette: aubergine and charcoal, amber primary actions, violet magic, ivory labels, teal support effects. Low-poly characters, props, and environments establish the presentation direction.

24 boards: a collection cover, 20 paired screen/state explorations, and three reference sheets. The paired portrait compositions show mobile layouts in a landscape gallery image.

## Coverage

- Entry and progression: main menu, continue, hero selection/upgrades, campaign stage selection, camp hub.
- Play: thumb-based combat HUD, boss warnings, dodge, skill cooldowns, skill selection and upgrades.
- Loot: inventory, gear comparison, victory/collection, forge with insufficient/sufficient materials.
- Supporting loops: fixed-price shop and purchase review, quests, achievements, daily rewards.
- Recovery and preferences: defeat/retry, pause/leave confirmation, audio/gameplay settings, accessibility, mirrored controls, loading/empty states, connection and transaction feedback.
- References: component states, navigation/loot/rarity icons, touch prompts and gestures.

## Production handoff

Before sale, rebuild source components, standardize typography and icons, export individual assets, and implement/test the gameplay UI. Verify target sizes, thumb reach, safe areas, mirrored controls, cooldown legibility, localization, contrast, and reduced motion on real devices. These flattened concept images do not supply 3D models or functioning game systems. Delivery formats, licensing, pricing, and engine support remain to be finalized.
