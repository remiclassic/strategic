The headline application fee is rarely the entire cost of pursuing a second citizenship. Begin with eligibility and the outcome you want, then compare the complete process. A residence-by-investment permit is not itself citizenship.

## Price each route consistently

| Route to investigate | Costs and requirements to check |
| --- | --- |
| Ancestry or existing entitlement | Eligibility, records, translations, registration and document fees |
| Naturalization | Qualifying residence, permitted absences, language or other requirements, application and living costs |
| Citizenship by investment | Approved contribution or investment, due diligence, dependent fees, professional costs and holding conditions |

These are common categories to investigate, not an exhaustive statement of every country's citizenship law. Family circumstances and special provisions may affect the available routes.

## Separate contribution from total budget

Dominica's EDF minimum for a single applicant is US$200,000 before additional fees. Add the actual household's costs using the current official schedule rather than an old advertisement. [Dominica CBIU](https://www.cbiu.gov.dm/investment-options/economic-diversification-fund/).

Do not treat old European investment tables as passport price lists. The Court of Justice ruled against Malta's 2020 investor citizenship scheme in April 2025. [Judgment summary](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/SUM/?uri=celex%3A62023CJ0181).

## Count time and uncertainty

Ask what residence is required, what evidence you must supply, which costs are non-refundable, and whether dependents have separate eligibility rules. A published processing estimate is not an approval guarantee. Include the practical cost of relocation if living there is part of the route.

## Make the comparison usable

Create one row per route with: eligibility evidence, desired rights, complete cost, time commitments, obligations, official source, quote date, and unresolved questions. Get the unknowns assessed before committing substantial money.
