A UAE freezone company should fit the activities you intend to carry out. Begin with the license and operating requirements, then assess tax, banking, and any residence plans separately.

## Match the license to the work

Give the authority or proposed provider a precise description of your services, customers, and work locations. Confirm permitted activities, premises requirements, any mainland-business restrictions, and relevant approvals directly. Ask for the applicable rules in writing.

## Assess qualifying status

Freezone registration does not automatically produce a 0% tax result. A Qualifying Free Zone Person may receive 0% on qualifying income and 9% on taxable income that is not qualifying, subject to conditions and exclusions. Foreign customers alone do not settle the question. [FTA Free Zone Persons guide](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Guides/CT/Free%20Zone%20Persons%20-%2020%2005%202024%20final%20for%20GCD.pdf).

The rules for an individual conducting business are a separate issue. Annual business turnover exceeding AED 1 million can bring a natural person within corporate tax; wages and personal investment income are treated separately. [FTA natural-person guidance](https://tax.gov.ae/en/taxes/corporate.tax/corporate.tax.topics/basis.of.taxation.natural.person.aspx).

## Request a complete budget

Separate initial and recurring charges for the license, premises, any visas, accounting, audit, tax compliance, and provider services. Record their renewal dates and closure costs. Do not assume that a desk package alone meets every tax or operating condition.

## Check access and obligations abroad

Confirm account eligibility with the intended bank or payment provider. Ask what documentation and visits it requires. Separately assess obligations where owners live, work, and manage the business.

Decide only after the license, annual budget, account route, and compliance responsibilities form a workable plan. See the [incorporation comparison worksheet](/blog/best-countries-incorporate-online-business/) and [Freedom Library](/reading-guide/) for further reading.
