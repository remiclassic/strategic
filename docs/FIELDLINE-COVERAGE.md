# Fieldline coverage

Ninth collection: military / tactical combat, contemporary and realistic. 24 presentation mockups: overview, 20 screen studies, and three reference boards.

## Requested systems

- Restrained olive drab, tan and charcoal layouts with clear sans-serif typography.
- Minimal top compass and heading tape with directional markers.
- Weapon attachment customizer, gear comparison, armor plates, grenade hotbars and saved loadouts.
- Fictional tactical maps with coordinate grids, ping legends, team status and objectives.

## Extended coverage

Operations hub, mode selection, squad lobby, HUD alert variants, stash and inspection, squad roles, scoreboard, briefing, extraction, match results, progression, requisition review, communications, spectator and reconnect, settings and accessibility, loading and confirmations, component states, icons, and input references.

## Production handoff

These are concept boards, not delivered engine assets. Create editable text and components, transparent sprites, nine-slice frames, and complete interaction states before release. Normalize sample names, coordinates, compass tick spacing, unit labels, armor/health fill values, weights, credits, compatibility and progression against one game data model. Check map coordinates against marker positions, team readiness against action availability, and weapon labels against the final inventory. Test localization, contrast, HUD scaling, keyboard/controller focus, touch targets, safe areas, and reconnect behavior in the target game. All locations and game statistics are illustrative.

The storefront remains in planning, links to SliceForge, and keeps thumbnails in a full-page grid without an internal scrollbar.
