Your company can be registered in one place while you work in another. That is the beginning of a cross-border analysis, not the end. A useful structure supports your customers and operations while keeping its full obligations manageable.

## Start with facts about the business

Write down the owners and their residence, where work and management happen, customer locations, employees or contractors, payment needs, and projected revenue. Ask an adviser which countries may impose tax, registration, payroll, or reporting duties on those facts.

## Compare the arrangement, not one tax rate

| Option | Questions worth resolving |
| --- | --- |
| A local entity | Can it serve the customers and payment methods you need with less administration? |
| US LLC | What tax classification, state duties, foreign-owner reporting and banking eligibility apply? |
| UK LLP | How are members taxed and which accounts and filing duties apply? |
| Estonian company | How do distributions, owner payments, and management abroad affect obligations? |
| UAE freezone company | What activities are licensed and does the company actually meet qualifying-income conditions? |
| Another foreign entity | Which practical benefit justifies the additional legal systems and annual costs? |

For a foreign-owned US entity, review whether [Form 5472 reporting](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i5472) applies. A low tax bill does not imply no filings. For a UK LLP, start with the government's [setup and operating guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/set-up-and-run-a-limited-liability-partnership-llp).

Estonia's distributed-profit tax rate uses 22/78 of the net distribution from 2025. This is not an exemption for every payment or foreign obligation. [Estonian Tax and Customs Board](https://www.emta.ee/en/business-client/taxes-and-payment/income-and-social-taxes/income-tax-and-basic-exemption).

In the UAE, freezone registration alone does not establish a 0% result. Qualifying status and the kind of income matter. [FTA Free Zone Persons guide](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Guides/CT/Free%20Zone%20Persons%20-%2020%2005%202024%20final%20for%20GCD.pdf).

## Request an annual operating budget

Include formation, registered services, accounting, filings, licenses, insurance, banking, professional advice, and closure costs. Confirm account eligibility separately: incorporation does not guarantee that a bank or payment provider will accept the business.
