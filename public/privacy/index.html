Privacy Policy for Strategic Sloth Games

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile games published by Strategic Sloth LLC ("Strategic Sloth", "we", "us") handle information. It applies to the following games (each a "game") on Google Play and any other store where we publish them:

  • Hearth & Crown: Tavern Rush
  • Tropic Tumble

Strategic Sloth LLC is a Wyoming limited liability company. Our mailing address is Strategic Sloth LLC, 1021 E Lincolnway 526, Cheyenne, WY 82001, United States. Our website at strategicsloth.com has its own website privacy policy. This page covers the games only.

Information stored on your device

Each game saves your progress and settings locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. Depending on the game, this can include levels or shifts completed, in-game coins, stars and rewards earned through play, upgrades, unlocked items, boosters, achievements, and sound, music and vibration settings. This information stays on your device. We never receive it.

Hearth & Crown: Tavern Rush may offer to export your progress to a backup file and to import a backup you choose. Backup files contain only game progress and stay wherever you save them. The game does not upload them.

If Android device backup is turned on in your Google account, Android may include a game's saved progress in your device backup. That backup is operated by Google under its own terms, not by us.

Hearth & Crown: Tavern Rush

Hearth & Crown: Tavern Rush does not collect, transmit or share any personal or usage data. All artwork, fonts and game content are bundled inside the app, and the game plays fully offline.

  • The game does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera, photos or files.
  • If you leave haptics on, the game uses device vibration for gameplay feedback. Sound, music and haptics can be turned off in the game's settings.
  • If your device supports it, tapping the innkeeper may play spoken lines using your device's built-in text-to-speech. That feature is provided by your device's operating system under its own settings and terms. The game does not record audio.
  • If you buy the game on Google Play, Google processes that purchase under its own terms and privacy policy. We receive only the standard sales and payout reports that Google provides to developers, which do not identify you to us by name.

Tropic Tumble

In any version of Tropic Tumble that does not include advertising or in-app purchases, the game works like Hearth & Crown: Tavern Rush. It stores your progress and settings on your device, does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera, photos or files, and does not send personal or usage data to us or to third parties.

Tropic Tumble: advertising and in-app purchases

This section applies only if and when a version of Tropic Tumble enables advertising or in-app purchases. It does not apply to Hearth & Crown: Tavern Rush. If a version of Tropic Tumble does not show ads or offer purchases, nothing in this section happens. The Google Play store listing and Data safety section for Tropic Tumble will show whether the current version contains ads or in-app purchases.

Advertising

If Tropic Tumble shows ads, they are provided by a third-party advertising network such as Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the ad provider's software in the game may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (on Android, the Google advertising ID) and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (such as country or city) may be inferred;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, app version and network type;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, tapped or rewarded; and
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data from the ad software.

The ad provider processes this information under its own privacy policy. For Google AdMob, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your name, email address or precise location from the ad provider. We receive aggregated reports about ad performance and earnings.

Your choices about ads.

  • Where the law requires consent (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game will ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using a consent tool such as Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice later from the game's settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings (usually Settings, then Privacy or Google, then Ads).
  • If you decline personalized ads, you may still see non-personalized ads, which use limited information such as approximate location and device type.
  • If Tropic Tumble offers a "Remove ads" purchase and you buy it, the game stops requesting ads on that Google Play account.

In-app purchases

If Tropic Tumble offers in-app purchases, they are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment details, billing address and payment method under its own terms and privacy policy. We never see or store your card number or other payment details.

To deliver what you bought and to restore purchases, the game and our systems may receive purchase information from Google Play, such as the product purchased, an order ID, a purchase token, the purchase time and the purchase status (for example completed, pending or refunded). We use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) to process in-app purchase data (purchase receipts, an anonymous app user ID, product and transaction information) to validate purchases and restore them. See RevenueCat privacy policy. We keep purchase records for as long as needed to provide purchases, handle refunds and meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Purchases are optional. Refund requests for Google Play purchases are handled under Google Play's refund policies.

Children

If Tropic Tumble enables advertising, we will configure the ad provider in line with Google Play's Families policies and applicable law. For users identified as children, or where we are required to treat users as children, ads will not be personalized and the ad provider's child-directed settings will be applied.

How we use and share information

Hearth & Crown: Tavern Rush gives us no information about you, so there is nothing for us to use or share. For Tropic Tumble, if ads or purchases are enabled, information is processed only for the purposes described above: showing and measuring ads, preventing fraud, providing and restoring purchases, handling refunds, and meeting legal obligations. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our legal rights. If Strategic Sloth LLC is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, the games and any related records could be transferred, and this policy would continue to apply to them.

Deleting your data

Clearing a game's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the game, removes its saved progress from your device. Delete any exported backup files separately if you no longer want them. Because our games have no accounts, we hold no game progress about you on our side. To request deletion of any purchase records we may hold for Tropic Tumble, email us with the Google Play order ID. We will delete what we are not legally required to keep.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because our games collect little or no personal information, most requests can be handled through the device and ad settings described above. You can also contact us at the email address below. We may need to verify your request and will respond within the time required by applicable law.

Security

Game progress is stored in each game's private app storage on your device. Information processed by Google or other providers is protected under their own security practices. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we limit the information our games handle to what is described in this policy.

Changes to this policy

If we add new online features, advertising, purchases, analytics or other data practices to any of our games, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above. Material changes will also be reflected in each game's Google Play Data safety section.
