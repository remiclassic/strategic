# Fantasy Adventurer: trailer comparison

Reviewed September 22, 2026 by seeking and visually inspecting key frames of Synty's 45-second Dark Fantasy Menus trailer: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h_Bx3uKKMUk&t=27s . Timestamps are approximate. This is a visual scope comparison, not a test of the competitor's delivered assets or implementation.

## Priorities

First add five focused mockups: loading, difficulty, pause/radial menu, expanded controls, and input/navigation glyphs. These close the clearest reusable menu-system gaps.

Second add optional shop/currency, tile hub, and emblem/item-tier sheets where relevant to the target games. A multiplayer lobby, clan page, or leaderboard is a separate scope decision; the trailer's menu labels are not evidence of full supplied implementations.

Additional recommendations from our own UX review, not attributed to observed trailer frames: quit/overwrite/delete confirmations; death/retry; display and accessibility settings; controller rebinding; empty/error/offline states. Prioritize confirmations and failure recovery before expanding art themes.

## Biggest remaining product gap

Our 12 images are presentation concepts. Matching this trailer's breadth visually would still leave the production work: normalize every repeated control, author layered source, separate sprites and glyphs, define scalable borders, test states and navigation, verify engine import, and demonstrate an actual interactive flow. A short recorded menu interaction and a downloadable, tested sample would provide stronger proof of usability than further hero artwork alone.

Keep the green/brass woodland identity. Use the reference to identify component categories and presentation coverage; create our own designs and assets.
