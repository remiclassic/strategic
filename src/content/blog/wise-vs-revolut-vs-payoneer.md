The right account is the one that supports how your business gets paid and pays others, at a cost you can understand. A universal ranking hides the details that change the answer: your company's country, currency pair, transaction size, and payment method.

## Start with the job

Review Wise when evaluating international transfers and currency conversion. Review Revolut Business when evaluating account plans and team spending features. Review Payoneer when evaluating cross-border receiving and marketplace payment workflows. These are starting points for comparison, not promises that every feature is available to every business.

Check the providers' current terms for your location: [Wise pricing](https://wise.com/pricing/), [Revolut Business plans](https://www.revolut.com/business/business-account-plans/), and [Payoneer pricing](https://www.payoneer.com/pricing/). Select the business product and the correct country before comparing.

## Price the same month three times

Use your expected activity rather than a provider's headline fee:

1. List incoming payments by currency, amount, source, and method.
2. List outgoing transfers, currency conversions, and card spending.
3. Add subscription, setup, receiving, withdrawal, and transaction charges that apply.
4. Include exchange-rate differences, allowances, and charges after limits.
5. Record the final amount recipients receive, not just the transfer fee.

Save dated quotes. A cheaper plan is useful only if the transactions you need are supported.

## Ask about access and protection

Confirm that your entity, owners, residence, and activities are eligible. Read what happens during a compliance review and how to contact support. Check which legal entity holds the account and whether funds are deposits, safeguarded funds, or another arrangement.

Do not assume that a financial app's name establishes deposit insurance. In the US, the FDIC explains that a nonbank company itself is never FDIC-insured; possible protection for underlying deposits depends on the arrangement and conditions. [FDIC: banking with third-party apps](https://www.fdic.gov/consumer-resource-center/2024-06/banking-third-party-apps).

## Keep the setup manageable

Choose a primary workflow and document a backup payment method. More accounts also mean more reconciliation, credentials, and reporting. Start with the smallest arrangement that meets your needs, then reconsider when transaction volume or team requirements change.
