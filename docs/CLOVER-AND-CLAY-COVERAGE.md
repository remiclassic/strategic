# Clover & Clay coverage

Eighth collection: casual / cozy / farm and town simulation. There are 24 presentation boards: one overview, 20 screen studies and three references for components, icons and inputs.

## Core direction

Chunky rounded low-poly forms, honey-colored wood, cream clay panels, sage actions and pastel accents. Oversized dialogue boxes use character portraits and emotion badges. Calendar and HUD studies cover dates, four seasons, time and weather. Shop studies include buy/sell tabs, categorized inventories, quantities, gold totals, stock states and favorite-item protection.

## Complete screen set

Main menu; farmer and farm setup; farm HUD; dialogue and emotions; calendar and weather; backpack and storage; shop buying; sell basket; crop growth; crafting; cooking; animal care; friendship and gifts; journal; town map and opening hours; home decorating; town projects; fishing and discoveries; pause, saves and accessibility; day summary, loading and confirmations.

## Production handoff

These images are visual concepts, not editable engine assets. Build text and controls as editable components, extract and validate transparent sprites and nine-slice regions, and make complete state variants before release. Normalize illustrative dates, season lengths, prices, inventory slot counts, item quantities, progress fills, character identities and recipe timing against one data model. Check purchase/sale arithmetic, insufficient funds, full storage, cancellation/refunds, favorites, crop growth across seasons and save recovery. Validate localization, portrait expressions, contrast, readable dialogue scale, keyboard/controller navigation and mobile touch targets in the target engine.

The storefront remains in planning, links to SliceForge, and uses the existing full-page gallery without an internal thumbnail scrollbar.
