# Neon Breach — collection coverage

Fifth storefront collection: tactical sci-fi, cyberpunk, and shooter HUD concepts with chunky low-poly 3D artwork. 24 presentation boards: one cover, 20 screen/flow studies, and three component reference sheets.

## Visual direction

Faceted operatives and equipment; beveled gunmetal panels; cyan focus and selection; amber primary actions; magenta environmental lighting. HUD elements sit around the playfield with a clear central aiming area. Landscape gameplay covers first-person, third-person, and mobile touch variants.

## Production handoff still required

These raster presentations establish the direction; editable components, exports, game logic, networking, and engine integration are not delivered by the mockups. Before release, normalize terminology, numerical examples, operative roles, glyph mappings, and component spacing across boards. Build real hover/focus/pressed/disabled variants, layered frames, transparent icons, and scalable panels. Validate ammo and health readability during combat, HUD safe areas, localization, controller focus, touch ergonomics, reduced motion, and non-color status cues in-engine.
