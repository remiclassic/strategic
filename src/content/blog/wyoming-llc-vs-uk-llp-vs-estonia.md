These names describe different legal arrangements. The useful comparison starts with your owners, where work happens, how customers pay, and how money reaches you. Formation cost alone is too narrow a basis for choosing.

## US LLC: classify before calculating

Ask how the LLC would be treated for tax, what state duties apply, and which foreign-owner disclosures are required. For relevant foreign-owned US entities, [Form 5472 rules](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i5472) can matter even when an owner expects little or no US income tax. Verify bank and payment-provider acceptance separately.

## UK LLP: understand the members' duties

Review the membership arrangement, accounts, registration, and filing responsibilities. Members' tax positions need an assessment based on their circumstances and the business activity. Start with [UK government LLP guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/set-up-and-run-a-limited-liability-partnership-llp).

## Estonian OÜ: model distributions and management

The corporate tax rate on distributed profits uses 22/78 of the net distribution from 2025. This does not settle salary, benefits, or obligations where an owner works or manages the business. [Estonian Tax and Customs Board](https://www.emta.ee/en/business-client/taxes-and-payment/income-and-social-taxes/income-tax-and-basic-exemption).

## Use one comparison sheet

For each option, record formation and closure costs, annual administration, owner-payment treatment, foreign obligations, account eligibility, public disclosures, and professional support. Ask the same questions using the same expected revenue and expenses. Include a local structure as a comparison.

The best result is an arrangement you can operate and maintain for a clear business benefit. A foreign registration does not remove personal obligations or guarantee access to customers and accounts.
