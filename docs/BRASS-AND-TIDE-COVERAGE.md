# Brass & Tide coverage

Tenth collection: pirate / high seas adventure. 24 concept boards: overview, 20 screen studies, and three component references.

## Requested features

Aged parchment, brass compass rings, rope trims and wooden planking. Ship HUDs cover hull integrity, sail angle, wind direction and cannon reload timers. Treasure maps show active, changed and completed markers; sea charts add coordinate grids, waypoints and legends. Bounty logs track objectives and rewards. Shipyard and crew screens cover part comparisons, upgrades, hiring fees, wages, assignments and morale.

## Extended coverage

Main menu and harbor, voyage setup, instrument reference, damage alerts, cargo inspection, ship appearance presets, repairs and crafting queues, port trading, fleet management, island discoveries, voyage results, saves, accessibility, empty states, confirmations, nautical icons, quickbars and input prompts.

## Production handoff

Presentation concepts only; editable engine assets are still to be built. Recreate live text and controls, transparent sprites and nine-slice frames. Normalize sample ship identities, hull and cargo fills, angle ranges, wind conventions, compass directions, map coordinates, reload timing, inventory quantities and crew capacity against one game data model. Check upgrade and trade totals, recurring wages, cancellation/refunds and marker transitions. Validate localization, contrast, keyboard/controller focus, touch targets and target-engine behavior before release. Dynamic marker behavior is illustrated, not implemented in these raster previews.

The storefront remains in planning, has a SliceForge referral link, and uses a full-page thumbnail grid without an internal scrollbar.
